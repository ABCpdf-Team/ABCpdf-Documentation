[The ISO PDF Specification, ISO 32000-1:2008 PDF 1.7; Table: 210, page 426.](https://opensource.adobe.com/dc-acrobat-sdk-docs/standards/pdfstandards/pdf/PDF32000_2008.pdf#page=434)

A hide action shows or hides one or more annotations by setting or clearing their Hidden flag. The T entry identifies the targets - a single reference, an array of references, or a field name string - and H selects whether to hide or show.
